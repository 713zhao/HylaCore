// FAQ

Good questions.

What exactly does HylaCore sell?

Professional AI services: consulting, the design and development of custom AI software, agents and automation, and ongoing operation and support of those systems. All services are delivered digitally.

How is pricing decided?

After a free initial conversation we send a written quote covering scope, deliverables, timeline and price. Projects are billed by milestone; retainers are billed monthly in advance. Nothing is charged before you accept a quote.

How do I pay?

We issue an invoice or payment link and you pay online by credit or debit card. Card details are handled entirely by our payment processor and are never stored on our systems.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Yes. Retainers can be cancelled at any time before the next billing date, and unstarted work is refundable. The full details are in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Who owns the work and the data?

You do. Once an invoice is paid in full, the deliverables created for you are yours, and your data is used only to deliver your project. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I get started?

Send us a short message describing what you would like to achieve. We reply by email, usually within two business days.
